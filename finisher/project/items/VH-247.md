# VH-247 — The Finisher's words in the translation register every tool shares, the student edition's with them

## Intent

The Finisher's translator's sheet (VH-105) is the Finisher's alone, and the Chinese and Malay pages are still machine drafts. Colleagues at the campuses will check the translations, and Name Graphics needs the same three languages. On the owner's word of 2026-11-26, the words of every tool go into one register, owned by the parent ledger. This item moves the Finisher onto that register: its English table goes in, its reviewed translations come back out, and its own sheet and scripts give way to the shared ones.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every message in the Finisher's English table is in the parent's register with its key, where it shows and its translator's note, tagged as the Finisher's (and as the student edition's where it shows only there); a test fails if a key is missing or out of step.
- The words drawn into the video (the title card's and the closings' text) are in it too, marked as drawn, since a change there changes the rendered video.
- The zh-Hans and ms-MY tables are written from the register by the parent's script; `src/i18n/sheet.ts`, `scripts/strings-sheet.mjs` and `scripts/strings-table.mjs` are retired or reduced to a call to it, and no table is edited by hand.
- Each language's "machine translated" note shows until every one of its rows is reviewed, and goes when they are (VH-105.2's last step, by rule, not by hand).
- The English for every machine-drafted row is recorded in the register as already translated once, so the first request asks the colleagues to review the drafts, not translate from scratch.
- `npm run check` green from `finisher/` and from the root.
