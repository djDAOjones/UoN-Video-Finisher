1. **What is false.** The numbers are readable lengths, not invented measurements. Mediabunny drops the incomplete Matroska cluster and reports what remains; `inspect.ts:341` takes the longer track duration. Thus “Duration 20 seconds” or “Duration 5 seconds” is numerically true of the readable remainder, but misleading as an unqualified statement of the original recording’s length—the precise distinction VH-158.1 requires.

   “Keeping the whole video” and the finished record’s “the whole video” falsely imply completeness under that criterion. Internally, `null` means no user trim; that implementation convention does not make the user-facing claim true.

   The missing finished warning is an omission, not a false sentence. “Your video is ready” can be true while concealing that its source was incomplete. The cut-short warning itself is true; only its proposed cause is uncertain. WebM’s before-Create state remains **unread**, although its code path predicts the same problem.
