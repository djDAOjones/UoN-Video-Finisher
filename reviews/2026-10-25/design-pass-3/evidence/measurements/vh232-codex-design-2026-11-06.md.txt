**1. Remedy and its boundaries**

The defect is established: the permitted g4 probe, `g4.vh222-probe-vh222-base-g4.jsonl:117`, puts English S-79a’s status at y=498–603; `:153` puts S-79b’s at y=498–687 and explicitly records clipping by `#feedback-dialog`. The complete text is present, including the address and omission sentence. This supports the ruled **Nielsen visibility defect**, without reclassifying it as a demonstrated WCAG failure. See also the permitted table `vh222-warning-rows-2026-11-05.md:99` and blind verdict `vh222-codex-blind.md:98–100`.

The builder’s criteria should be:

- After each non-empty status paint, measure the rendered status against the dialog’s **client scrollport**, excluding borders and scrollbars and intersected with the visible viewport.
- If the status fits and is already wholly visible, do nothing.
- Otherwise, scroll the dialog vertically by the smallest distance that brings its whole status box into view. Below the scrollport means reveal its bottom; above means reveal its top. Clamp to the dialog’s actual scroll range.
- Use an **instant** scroll, never smooth animation. Do not use unrestricted `scrollIntoView()`, which can also scroll ancestors.
- Do not call `focus()`, add `tabindex`, replace the status node, change its role, or reinsert it to force an announcement.
- Do not change the document’s scroll position, the dialog’s horizontal scroll, its dimensions or positioning rules, DOM order, spacing, typography, controls or disclosure state. The dialog’s contents necessarily move together on screen when its scroll offset changes; their layout positions relative to one another do not.
- Preserve ordinary text-driven height changes already produced by the existing CSS. The remedy must not introduce an additional resize.

The status remains after the actions, as [index.html:900–907](/Users/uazjob/UoN-Video-Helper-side/vh232/index.html:900) defines it.

**Over-height status:** no scrolling algorithm can put text taller than the scrollport wholly in view simultaneously. Align its beginning with the scrollport’s top, retain every word, and leave ordinary vertical scrolling available through its end. Do not oscillate between its edges, truncate it or shrink the type. Record this as the over-height fallback, **not** as a “whole and in view” pass. All normal required matrix cells must meet the stronger whole-visible criterion; a normal cell that cannot fit blocks acceptance and requires reconsideration.

**Focus:** unchanged `activeElement` is necessary but insufficient. Measure the focused control and its indicator after the reveal. The ordinary submit/copy cases must keep them visible. Also test a delayed copy response after the user has moved focus elsewhere in the dialog. If the focused control and complete status cannot fit together, the item must expose that conflict; it must not silently claim AAA compliance or move focus to conceal it.

**Empty status:** no explicit reveal or scroll reset. Opening continues to clear the previous status before `showModal()` ([main.ts:3707](/Users/uazjob/UoN-Video-Helper-side/vh232/src/main.ts:3707)). Any browser clamping caused by content becoming shorter is distinguished from an authored scroll.

**Closed dialog and asynchronous completion:** a late clipboard result must neither reopen nor scroll the closed dialog, nor populate a later opening with an old result. Associate pending copy work with the opening that started it; discard its UI result if that opening has ended, including close-and-reopen before completion. Both Close and Escape must invalidate it. This covers the asynchronous sets at `main.ts:3791` and `:3796`.

**Language change:** reveal from inside the registered paint callback, after the new text is assigned, so the translated status is measured afresh. Preserve the existing language-switch announcement policy: the switch deliberately quietens live regions during repaint; do not make feedback speak independently over it (`main.ts:3863–3892`, `:3921–3924`).

I reject the alternatives for this item:

| Option | Reason |
|---|---|
| Move the status earlier in the DOM | Changes reading order and layout; still does not guarantee visibility in a scrolled dialog. |
| Sticky status | Can cover controls and focus, and consumes substantial space at 320 px. |
| Shorter text | Needs owner-approved wording and risks losing recovery information. |
| Taller dialog | Already capped near the viewport height; cannot solve the narrow-window case reliably. |
| Dialog-only reveal | Directly addresses the missing visibility with the smallest behavioural change. |
