**2. Cause and rule**

The failure is lost perimeter, not insufficient ring colour contrast. At the old offset, a 2 px outline extends 4 px beyond each fragment. Consecutive fragments commonly have a **7 px gap**: the outlines meet, and Chrome paints a joined contour with interior horizontal edges missing.

For example, VH-203’s raw b5 Malay zoom400proxy record reads **986/1,236 px²**. The before captures show the joined contour; the candidate captures show separate outlines.

`outline-offset: 0` reduces each outline’s outward extent to 2 px, retaining those interior edges without changing line layout. It is the smallest sound remedy demonstrated here. The design trials reinforce that choice: cloning decorations did not repair the failures; increasing width with `--focus-room` still left service-link failures.

The selector matches **any focused-visible anchor descended from any `.statement-body`**, not just these two IDs. Today those are the contact and service links. A future link within that class also receives the rule automatically, including one nested deeper in the markup.

Its specificity, `(0,2,1)`, beats global `:focus-visible`, `(0,1,0)`, **for offset only**:

- Dark and blue-band contexts retain their existing focus colour.
- Forced colours retain `Highlight`; the new rule declares no colour.
- Ordinary mouse focus that does not match `:focus-visible` is unaffected. Browser focus-visible heuristics still govern.
- An unwrapped link gets the same 2 px solid outline moved inward by 2 px. There is no unwrapped specimen in these 126 readings, so that conclusion is from the CSS, not a separately walked case.

**3. Tokens**

Carbon’s **“Tokens, not literals”** rule is satisfied.
