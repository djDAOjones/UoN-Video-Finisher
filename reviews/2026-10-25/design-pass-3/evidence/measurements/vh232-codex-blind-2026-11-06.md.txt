**2. Geometry**

[reveal-status.ts:116](/Users/uazjob/UoN-Video-Helper-side/vh232/src/ui/reveal-status.ts:116) correctly places the client top at `rect.top + clientTop`, excluding the border. Its client bottom is that top plus `clientHeight`.

- **Already visible:** returns before padding or focus adjustments. Thus an already visible status does not move merely to acquire padding.
- **Clipped above:** aligns the status start with the client top—the least upward movement that reveals its start.
- **Clipped below:** requests the bottom overflow plus computed end padding, capped at the movement that would align the start. This avoids buying padding by clipping the status’s beginning.
- **Exact fit:** an already aligned box does not move. A displaced exact-height box is aligned at its start; padding necessarily yields.
- **Over-height:** aligns the start, subject to focus and scroll-range constraints. Repetition settles instead of alternating between start and end.
- **Invalid padding:** non-finite parsing becomes zero; negative values are also bounded at zero.
- **Range:** the final assignment is clamped to `[0, scrollHeight − clientHeight]`.

The focus interval is sound: its lower bound keeps the ring’s bottom above the client bottom; its upper bound keeps the ring’s top below the client top. Projecting the requested scroll onto that interval gives the nearest focus-preserving scroll. A control taller than the client area has no such interval; the helper explicitly leaves the status target unchanged in that case.

Fractional arithmetic is retained, and the return value reads back the actual assigned scroll. **Exact mathematical containment is not guaranteed under scroll quantisation.** In a rounded stand-in, an exact-height status requiring `scrollTop=200.4` can settle at 200, leaving 0.4 px outside; repeated calls report zero movement rather than oscillating. The probe permits 0.5 px tolerance. I found no measured cell failing because of this, but the fractional unit test does not establish rounded behaviour.
