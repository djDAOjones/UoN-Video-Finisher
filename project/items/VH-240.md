# VH-240 — Step 6 keeps its height while the device check runs again

## Intent

Analyst A's first finding (A-01): any trim or output change withdraws the verdict while the check runs again, so step 6 loses 422 px for up to a second, the Create button vanishes and comes back, and near the foot of the page everything bounces. UI-STANDARDS asks that loading states keep the layout still. VH-124's rule stays: no stale verdict is shown above "Checking…"; only the space is held.

## Acceptance criteria

- While a re-check is running, step 6 is no shorter than it was when the check began; the hold is released when the new verdict, a refusal or a failure is painted, and on a new file.
- No stale verdict, estimate or audio note is visible or exposed to a screen reader during the check.
- Measured in Chrome before and after: step 6's height and the page's scroll height through a re-check, at 1280 px and 375 px.
- No word, sentence, key or control added or changed in any language; no `docs/` line; no runtime dependency; no test deleted, skipped or weakened.
- `npm run check` green with no new skip; read in Chrome on the MacBook Pro, the reading recorded; what only a person or the Mac Pro's walk can read is named, not claimed.
