# VH-231 — A cut Matroska or WebM file is said cut short with the finished video, and its read length is never called the whole video

## Intent

VH-222's readings on the Mac Pro found VH-158.1's case. A Matroska or WebM file that is cut short is warned about before Create: "The video file is shorter than expected", first in `#audio-warnings` and spoken in `#status`. After Create the warning is nowhere on the page. `withdrawVerdict()` empties `#audio-warnings` when the job ends (`src/main.ts`, `:995`, `:3173`). The card's warnings come only from `reply.outputWarnings` (`:3187`, painted at `:3460`), and these hold only the production warnings (`src/workers/job.worker.ts`, `:289`; `detectProductionWarnings`, `src/audio/warnings.ts`, `:225`–`:236`). `endedEarly` finds nothing, because Mediabunny's Matroska index lists only what it could read (`src/media/matroska-cut.ts`, `:4`–`:7`). So the record says "the whole video (20 seconds)" with nothing beside it, and a person checking the page after Create is told nothing was lost. The trim line ("Keeping the whole video, 20 seconds.") also calls the readable part the whole video. This item says the file is cut short with the finished video too, its announcement and the retained result, and it gives the trim and the record the length kept, in the owner's approved words.
