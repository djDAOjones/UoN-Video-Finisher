# VH-241 — Eight readings mended in the stylesheet: the skip link on the blue band, the focus ring at the window's edge, reduced motion's still band, and five smaller ones

## Intent

The CSS-only fixes the first analysis found, each on existing tokens: the skip link is blue on the blue band in light, its box and ring both 1.00:1 (B-01); a control reached by Tab at the window's bottom edge has part of its ring outside the window (wish VH-233.1, B-10, A-18); under reduced motion the indeterminate progress bar rests as an empty track, not the still band its comment promises (B-02); in dark an invalid field's error line is nearly the helper's colour (A-04); under text spacing at 320 px the Malay "Set start here" button leaves its panel (B-05); the footer's two help links run together when they wrap (B-08); the Report a problem dialog jumps upward when its details open (A-14) and hides its buttons at 320 × 568 (B-04); and eight student IDs do not all show in their box (B-07).

## Acceptance criteria

- The skip link takes the blue band's roles: its box, words and ring each at least 3:1 against the band in light and dark, its words at least 7:1 on its box.
- The page keeps a scroll inset from an existing token, so a control focused at the window's top or bottom edge shows its whole ring.
- Under reduced motion an indeterminate bar rests as a visible band.
- A field's error line is told from its helper line without colour, by an existing type role.
- The trim's two Set buttons wrap inside their panel under text spacing at 320 px; the footer's help links have space between them when they wrap; the dialog grows downward and, at 320 px wide, shows more of its buttons; the student IDs box grows with what is typed.
- `test/stylesheet.test.ts` and `test/contrast.test.ts` pass, every new length a token.
- No word, sentence, key or control added or changed in any language; no `docs/` line; no runtime dependency; no test deleted, skipped or weakened.
- `npm run check` green with no new skip; read in Chrome on the MacBook Pro, the reading recorded; what only a person or the Mac Pro's walk can read is named, not claimed.
