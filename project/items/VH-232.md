# VH-232 — The feedback dialog's status is brought whole into view when it is set

## Intent

VH-222's readings on the Mac Pro found that the problem report's status can be set out of sight. In "Report a problem", `setFeedbackStatus` (`src/main.ts`, `:3657`–`:3661`) writes the status's text and nothing else. `#feedback-status` is the form's last child, after the actions (`index.html`, `:907`), and the dialog is `overflow: auto`, at most the window's height less 32 px (`src/styles/app.css`, `.dialog`, `:1641`–`:1650`). Where the dialog's content is taller than that, the new status lands below the visible area. In S-79a's 3 cells at 320 px in light, and S-79b's 14 (320 px in all four modes, and 375 px light in en-GB and ms-MY), only its first two lines show. The row's own sentence, the details left out or the address to write to, lies wholly below, and nothing reveals it. The orchestrator ruled it a defect under Nielsen's "status visible and announced", not a WCAG failure: 1.4.10 and 4.1.3 pass. This item brings the whole status into view when it is set, by the least scroll of the dialog alone. Focus, the words, the elements and the announcement stay as they are.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The reveal.** When `setFeedbackStatus` writes a non-empty text and `#feedback-dialog` is open, the dialog's `scrollTop` moves by the least vertical distance that brings the status's whole box into the dialog's client area. Where the status is already wholly in view, nothing moves. The distance comes from DOM geometry: the status's box against the dialog's client area, clamped to the dialog's scroll range. The room kept below the status is the dialog's own end padding, read from its computed style (`--spacing-07`, `src/styles/tokens.carbon.css`, `:38`). The scroll is instant: no smooth behaviour, and no `scroll-behavior` in the styles.
- **Only the dialog scrolls.** No `scrollIntoView`, and no ancestor scroll: the page behind keeps `scrollX` and `scrollY`, and the dialog keeps `scrollLeft`. Nothing else moves: the dialog's size and position, the DOM order, every element's place inside the dialog's content, the disclosure's state, the type and the spacing. `src/styles/app.css` is unchanged.
- **Focus.** Nothing calls `focus()`, adds a `tabindex`, or replaces the node. `document.activeElement` is the same before and after. In every walked cell, the focused control (the button pressed: Open email app, or Copy message and details) stays wholly in view with its ring. A matrix cell that cannot keep both the whole status and the focused control in view stops the build: the remedy outgrows the item (see Sized).
- **Announced as now.** `#feedback-status` stays the same `<p class="status" role="status" aria-live="polite">`, and its text is still written by `textContent`. A language switch's quietening of the live regions (`src/main.ts`, `:3863`, `:3921`–`:3924`) is unchanged.
- **Where it runs.** The reveal runs inside the paint callback that `show()` registers (`src/main.ts`, `:276`), after the text is written, so a repainted translation is measured afresh. It is guarded by an open dialog and a non-empty text:
  - an emptied status (`openFeedback`, `:3708`) is not revealed: the dialog opens at its top, with focus on the message field, as now;
  - a closed dialog never scrolls, Copy's late result included (`:3790`–`:3797`). The text that result writes is unchanged by this item: this item's wish line on it is in the wish list.
  - all four sets (`:3772`, `:3780`–`:3782`, `:3791`, `:3796`) take the reveal through `setFeedbackStatus`, and none is otherwise changed.
- **Taller than the visible area.** No cell of the matrix reaches this case; it may occur at the 32 px font or a zoom proxy. The status's start is aligned with the top of the client area, every word kept, and the rest is reached by scrolling. Repeated calls do not oscillate. The table records it as this fallback, never as "whole and in view".
- **The code.** A small helper of its own in `src/ui/` (not in `feedback.ts`'s report functions), tested on stand-ins as `src/ui/focus-hand-on.ts` is, called from `setFeedbackStatus`. No number outside `src/config/` or a CSS token, and no new token.
- **Words and elements.** No new or changed string in any language, no `docs/` line, and no element, control or checkbox. So there is no wording wish. Nothing in the spec is made false (`docs/01-specification.md`, `:682`–`:688`).
- **Tests.** The helper's stand-in tests cover:
  - already visible; clipped below; clipped above; an exact fit; fractional geometry; clamping to the scroll range;
  - over-height, with repeated calls and no oscillation;
  - an empty text, a closed dialog and a disconnected node: no scroll;
  - no `focus()`, no ancestor or horizontal scroll, and no change to the content;
  - a replaced, translated text measured afresh;
  - Copy's delayed result after Close, after Escape, after a close and reopen, and with focus moved by the user, each outcome recorded.

  `src/ui/feedback.test.ts` stays intact. The scroll, the keys and the announcement are proved in Chrome by the walk.
