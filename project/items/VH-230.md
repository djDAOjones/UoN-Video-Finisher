# VH-230 — The state words broken in the System check's mark column

## Intent

The System check's mark column is 1.5 rem, too narrow for some of its state words, so each breaks over two lines: at the default font Malay's "Tidak" (36.33 of 24 px) and Chinese's 好的 (28 of 24 px); under the spacing sheet "OK" (24.38 of 24 px), and those two wider; Chinese's pending "……", width unmeasured. At 32 px on 1280 px "Tidak" reads 72.64 and 好的 56 of 48 px; at 32 px on a phone VH-218's rule ended it. The same before and after VH-218. This item keeps each state word whole in its column, on his "even old issues should be solved for".

## Acceptance criteria

- **Each mark whole on one line,** in every state and condition below, DOM order and words unchanged.
- **A remedy chosen, by the worker's design and Codex's, from the two named, unchosen here:** widen the gutter for every row, or add a token. The row's label keeps VH-218's result: no word broken.
- **Measured before and after,** at the matrix VH-218 walked for S-82: pending, passed, warned and failed; 1280, 375 and 320 px; default font, 32 px font and spacing mode; en-GB, zh-Hans and ms-MY; light, dark, forced light and forced dark: 432 cells a side. Each mark's lines and width, the ellipsis measured.
- **VH-203's stage rule, kept whole,** as [VH-218](VH-218.md) lists it: WCAG re-checked for what it touched; tokens on the two token files, never collapsed, no literal outside `src/config/` or a token, no control or element added; the gate; Codex astra's check; the report's Disposition row linked.
- **Lands before VH-220,** whose checks read the final page.
